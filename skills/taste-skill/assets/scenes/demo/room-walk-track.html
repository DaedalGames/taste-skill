Rooms that letthe city slow down

We build stairs, halls, stations and libraries for towns that want somewhere to sit without paying for it. Walk through eight of our rooms, left to right.

Light comes infrom above

The Abbey Library in Ghent keeps its old vaults and adds nothing at eye level. Daylight falls from the high windows, so the books stay out of the sun and the readers sit in it.

We start every project on the stairs. A stair is where strangers slow down together, look up and decide how far to go. Get the stair right and the rest of the building tends to follow it.

Who says a public roomhas to be loud?

Most of our clients are towns, not companies. They arrive with a site, a small budget and a long list of the people who will use it. We arrive with time: a year of drawings, a year of models, and a day in every room.
