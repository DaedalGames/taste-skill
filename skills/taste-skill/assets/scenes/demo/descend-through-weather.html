Skarven

Brekka, eleven rooms on the ridge above the sound, open from May to October.

Inside the cloud, 800 metres up

Below the cloud, the whole sound opens.

Eleven rooms and one long table

Brekka sits at 1,140 metres on the north ridge. Most mornings the cloud fills the sound and burns off by nine; from then on you can count the boats. Every room faces the water.

Park at Skarvik, the village on the coast road, two hours by car from Moldvik. From there a stone path, forty minutes on foot; the goods lift takes your bags.
