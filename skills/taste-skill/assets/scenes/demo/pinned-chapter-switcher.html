Swim the open sea

A sea-swimming school on the outer coast, with guided swims from seven until dark and a wood-fired sauna by the water.

One day at the baths

  1. Morning
  2. Sundown
  3. Last watch

In off the rocks

The first swim leaves at seven from the flat rocks below the bath house. The bay is clear and fourteen degrees, and a guide swims at the edge of every group.

Out past the break

When the evening swell comes in gold off the point, the strong swimmers go out past the break with a guide. Everyone else watches from the warm sand.

The last watch

When the light goes, a guide stands on the headland until every swimmer is in. Then the sauna, fish soup, a towel by the fire, and the gate locked at ten.

Open June to August

Three swims a day, and every swim has a guide in the water and a kayak alongside. Bring a swimsuit; we lend wetsuits, gloves and the orange caps.

  • 18 euros a swim, the sauna included
  • 1.2 kilometres across the bay, the longest swim
  • 16 degrees in the sea in a good August
