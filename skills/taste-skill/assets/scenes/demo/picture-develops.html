Fellside Architects

Houses for steep ground

Four architects in a former dairy above the fjord, drawing every house by hand on the slope where it will stand.

A house drawn into the hill

Every Fellside house begins on site with a pen. This sheet was drawn above Varde in one afternoon: three concrete floors stepping down the slope, each roof a terrace for the room above.

Building starts in April

The foundations go into the rock this winter. The stair, the terraces and the kitchen are being drawn now, and each sheet goes up here when it is finished.
